Every change a player or contributor would notice gets a line here, in the same
pull request as the change. New lines go under **Unreleased**.

## Unreleased

- **Back up Nord.** On a Stage 4 a backup now holds your samples and pianos
  too.
- **Update a backup….** A new button in the backup dialog: it copies the
  samples and pianos that haven't changed from an earlier backup and reads the
  rest from your Nord.
- **Restore Nord….** A restore puts your samples back by name. Pianos are
  still off until they have been tried on a Nord: until then they are left out
  and the summary says so.
- **Send to Nord for samples.** From a sample in the Library and from the
  **Save** dialog of Build sample. Now on: sending a sample has been tried on a
  Stage 4.
- **Linked folder.** In Chrome and Edge, files you add or save in the folder show
  up when you come back to OpenNord, without **Rescan folder**, and on opening
  OpenNord the folder's programs and presets show straight away. The reconnect
  banner says how to stop being asked (**Allow on every visit**), and browsers
  that can't remember a folder say so where you choose it.
- **Connecting a Stage 2.** It's called a Nord Stage 2 in the app and in the
  Check my Nord results, not just “Nord”.
- **Manifesto.** Removed from the website and the repository.
- **Website and docs.** Plainer wording, and every page now says the same thing
  about which Nords are tested: the Stage 4 and Stage 3 for everything, the
  Stage 2 for reading only, other models not yet.
- **Website.** The front page is shorter: what OpenNord does, the desktop app,
  and links into these docs for the detail.
- **Docs.** OpenNord has documentation: these pages, at `/docs/`, opened from
  **Docs** in the side rail or the ⌘K menu.
