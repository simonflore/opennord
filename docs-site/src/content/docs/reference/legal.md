## Not affiliated with Nord

OpenNord is **not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by or connected to
Clavia DMI AB or Nord Keyboards.** "Nord", "Nord Stage" and related names belong
to their owner and are used here only to describe what OpenNord works with.

## Your own work, never Nord's sounds

The line is **who made it**, not the file type:

- **Your programs and presets** describe settings. They refer to the samples
  and pianos they use, but contain no audio. They are your own work. ✅
- **Samples and pianos you made yourself** (for example, recorded from your own
  instruments) are your own work too. ✅
- **Nord's factory sounds and sample libraries** belong to their owner. OpenNord
  never hosts, sends or redistributes them. ❌

When OpenNord lists the factory samples a program uses, it names them and links
to Nord's own free downloads. It never ships the audio itself.

## Your own instrument, on your own machine

Reading anything from your own Nord to your own computer, factory sounds
included, and looking at it or editing it there, is personal use of your own
device, the same thing Nord Sound Manager does when it backs up your keyboard.
It stays on your machine.

## Your files stay with you

Your programs, samples and pianos are opened on your own device. OpenNord never
uploads them.
