**User Samples** in the Library holds your `.nsmp` (oldest), `.nsmp3` (Stage
3 era) and `.nsmp4` (Stage 4) samples: imported files, files in your folder,
and the samples on a connected Nord.

## Add samples

**+ Add** › **Import a file…** (imported samples stay in OpenNord),
**Choose a folder…**, or **Build a sample…** to make one from your own
recordings ([Build a sample](/docs/guide/build-sample/)).

## Look at a sample

Open a sample to see its keyboard map, root notes and loops, and the list of
recordings (strokes) it holds.

## Play it

- Play on the on-screen keyboard, or press **Connect MIDI keyboard** and play
  from a MIDI keyboard (Chrome or Firefox on a computer, the desktop app, or
  the iPad app).
- **Unison** (Off, 1, 2, 3) and **Envelope** (attack, decay, sustain, release)
  are for listening here only; they are not saved in the sample.

## Edit the keyboard map

Under **Edit · Keyboard map**, change root notes and splits (and on newer files
velocity, gain, detune and the name), then **Save edited copy**. Your original
stays as it was.

The oldest `.nsmp` files can be read-only: convert them to `.nsmp3` or
`.nsmp4` first, then edit that.

## Convert and export

- **Convert** makes an `.nsmp4`, `.nsmp3` or `.nsmp` copy for another
  generation of Nord. These are marked "Experimental: not yet tried on a Nord".
- **Make it a piano** turns a sample into a `.npno` piano (experimental).
- **WAV** on a stroke downloads that recording; **Export all (.zip)** downloads
  every one.
- **Save to computer** downloads the sample file itself.

Factory samples can be opened and played too. They stay on your device;
OpenNord never shares them.

## Free up space on your Nord

With a Nord connected:

1. Press **Find unused samples**: OpenNord checks which samples no program on
   the keyboard uses. **Unused only** lists them.
2. Select the ones to remove and press **Delete from Nord**. The dialog says
   how much space it frees.
3. Tick **Save a copy to a folder first** to keep them.

Factory samples you delete can be downloaded again from Nord's library.
