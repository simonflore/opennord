## Pianos

**Piano Samples** lists the `.npno` pianos in your folder, in backups there,
and on a connected Nord.

- Open one to play it under **Listen · Keyboard**, with velocity, note by note
  or from a MIDI keyboard.
- **Save to computer** downloads it (from the Nord, it is read off the
  keyboard first). A piano inside a backup needs **Load sounds** first.
- **+ Add** › **Make a piano from a sample…** turns one of your samples into a
  piano (experimental).
- With a Nord connected, **Find unused pianos** and **Delete from Nord** work as
  for [samples](/docs/guide/samples/).

There is no single-file import for pianos: put `.npno` files in your folder.

## Presets

**Presets** holds organ, piano and synth presets: `.ns4o`, `.ns4n`, `.ns4y`
(Stage 4), `.ns3y` (Stage 3) and `.ns2s` (Stage 2). Import them, read them from
a folder or from your Nord, and filter by type (**Organ**, **Piano**, **Synth**)
and model.

Open a preset to see its settings. Some are recognised but not yet shown in
detail. Presets can be saved to your computer, but not sent to the Nord yet.
