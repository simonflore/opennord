---

OpenNord is a free, open-source app for Nord keyboards. It opens Nord program,
sample and piano files and shows their settings. With a USB cable it can copy
programs to and from the keyboard and back it up.

It is beta software. It has been tested on the Nord Stage 4 and Stage 3; other
models are untested. See [Supported instruments](/docs/reference/instruments/).
