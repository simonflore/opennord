You don't need a keyboard to start. A program file you exported from Nord
Sound Manager, or one from the Nord User Forum, is enough.

## Open a program file

1. Open OpenNord. It starts on the **Library**, on **Programs**.
2. Press **+ Add** and pick **Import a file…**.
3. Choose a Nord Stage program: `.ns4p` (Stage 4), `.ns3f` or `.ns3p` (Stage 3)
   or `.ns2p` (Stage 2).
4. The program opens on the right: its keyboard zones, one card per engine
   (piano, organ, synth), its effects and the samples it uses.

OpenNord keeps a copy of an imported file, so it is still there after a reload.
It stays in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

## Or connect a folder

If you keep your Nord files in a folder, press **+ Add** › **Choose a folder…**
instead. OpenNord reads the files where they are (subfolders and `.ns4b`
backups too) and copies nothing. See [The Library](/docs/guide/library/).

## Or connect your Nord

On a computer, in Chrome or Edge, plug the Nord in over USB and press
**Connect** in the top bar. The programs on the keyboard appear in the Library
under **On Nord**. See [Connect your Nord](/docs/guide/connect/).

## Find your way around

- The **rail** on the left: the Library lists (**Programs**, **Presets**,
  **User Samples**, **Piano Samples**), the sources, and the tools (**Device**,
  **Compatibility**).
- **⌘K** (Ctrl K on Windows) jumps to any program, sample or page by name.
- On a phone, the tabs at the bottom do the same, and **More** opens the rest.
