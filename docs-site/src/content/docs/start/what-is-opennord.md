OpenNord is a free, open-source app for **Nord® keyboards**. It runs in the
browser and as a desktop app for macOS and Windows. An iPad app is in progress.

It can:

- **Open program files** and show their settings: piano or sample, organ,
  synth, effects, keyboard split and the samples the program uses. You don't
  need the keyboard or Nord Sound Manager for this.
- **Keep a library** of the programs, presets, samples and pianos in a folder,
  in single files you import, or on a connected keyboard.
- **Play samples and pianos** on an on-screen keyboard or a MIDI keyboard, and
  export samples to WAV.
- **Copy programs to and from the keyboard** over USB, and back the keyboard up.
- **Convert a Stage 2 or 3 program** into a Stage 4 program.

## What has been tested

Everything above has been tested on the **Nord Stage 4** and **Nord Stage 3**.
On the **Stage 2**, reading files and copying from the keyboard have been
tested. Nothing has been tested on other models yet. The full table is in
[Supported instruments](/docs/reference/instruments/).

## What it is not

- It is **not made by Nord** and not endorsed by them. See [Legal](/docs/reference/legal/).
- It **doesn't share Nord's sounds.** Programs name the factory samples they
  use; the audio stays with Nord.
- It is **beta software**. Sending a program to the keyboard replaces what is in
  that slot. [Back up first](/docs/guide/backup/).

OpenNord is free and its source is
[on GitHub](https://github.com/simonflore/opennord) under the AGPL license.
