OpenNord is a free, open-source companion for **Nord® keyboards**, starting
with the **Nord Stage** line. It runs in your browser and as a desktop app for
macOS and Windows; an iPad app is on the way.

- **Read your programs.** Open a program file and see what's inside it: piano
  or sample, organ, synth, effects, key zones and the samples it uses. No
  keyboard and no Nord Sound Manager needed.
- **Organise a library.** Point OpenNord at a folder, or open single files, and
  it builds a searchable **Library** of your programs, presets, samples and
  pianos.
- **Play your samples.** Audition a sample on an on-screen keyboard, or from a
  MIDI keyboard on a computer, and export it to WAV.
- **Talk to your Nord.** Over USB, list what is on the keyboard, pull programs
  off it, write them back and back the keyboard up.
- **Bring old programs forward.** Turn a Nord Stage 2 or 3 program into one
  your Stage 4 can load.

## What it is not

- It is **not made by Nord** and not endorsed by them. See [Legal](/docs/reference/legal/).
- It **doesn't share Nord's sounds.** Programs refer to factory samples by
  name; the audio stays where Nord publishes it.
- It is **beta software.** Things change and break. Writing to your Nord
  replaces what is in that place, so [back up first](/docs/guide/backup/).

## Which Nords

The **Nord Stage 2, 3 and 4** are covered first, and the Stage 4 most fully.
Many other models' files are read too: Electro, Piano, Lead, Wave, Grand and
more. See [Supported instruments](/docs/reference/instruments/) for the full list.

## Free and open

OpenNord is free, and its source is
[open](https://github.com/simonflore/opennord) under the AGPL license.
